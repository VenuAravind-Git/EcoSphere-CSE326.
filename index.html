How to use EcoSphere

  1. Log in: type your name in the box below and press Login.
  2. Discover: move your mouse over the cards to learn about campus problems.
  3. Take the 7-Day Challenge: press the Done button on each mission you complete.
  4. Earn EcoPoints and badges: badges unlock as you finish missions.
  5. Imagine: read the Impact Wall and the Campus of 2035.

Our Campus, Our Planet

Common environmental problems on a campus (hover over a card):

Waste

Mixed waste cannot be recycled. Separate dry and wet waste.

Water

A leaking tap wastes many litres. Close taps fully.

Energy

Lights and fans left on in empty rooms waste power.

Green spaces

Trees give shade, clean the air and shelter birds.

Transport

Walking or cycling short distances saves fuel.

Why should you care?

One small habit looks tiny, but when many students repeat it every day it makes a big difference.
